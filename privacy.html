Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This policy describes the intended academic prototype. The public informational pages on this site do not themselves offer TikTok sign-in or collect TikTok account information.

1. Information processed

If integration features are enabled, the application may process information authorized by the connected platform and account holder, such as account identifiers, display names, profile information, public video metadata, and customer interaction records obtained through permitted methods. Access tokens may be processed to maintain authorized connections.

2. Purposes

Information is intended to be used for account connections, displaying permitted content, organizing customer interactions, debugging integrations, and evaluating optional message categorization features for academic research and demonstration.

3. Third-party services

Connected platforms process information under their own policies. If AI classification or external hosting is enabled, relevant content may be sent to configured service providers. Such integrations must be disclosed and configured before real personal data is processed.

4. Security and access

The project is intended to limit access to authorized users and protect credentials. Tokens and secrets must not be published in client-side code or public repositories. No internet-connected system can be guaranteed completely secure.

5. Retention and deletion

During development, test data should be retained only as needed for the academic evaluation and removed when no longer necessary. Account disconnection and deletion workflows must be implemented and verified before real-user deployment.

6. Your choices

Users can decline platform authorization. Where supported, they can revoke access through the connected platform's account settings. For data access or deletion requests, contact the developer.

7. Contact

Project developer: TachibanaJQK on GitHub. Before collecting real user data, this section should be updated with a monitored contact email and a working request process.

8. Updates

This policy may change when the application's data flows and integrations change. Revisions will be published here.
