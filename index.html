Unified Conversation Manager

An academic prototype for connecting supported social media accounts and organizing customer interactions in a unified dashboard.

Project overview

The project explores account authorization, retrieval of platform-permitted data, conversation organization, and optional AI-assisted categorization. Features are under development and depend on the permissions granted by each platform.
